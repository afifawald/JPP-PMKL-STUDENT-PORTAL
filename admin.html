How it connects

Cloudflare Pages & KV Setup in 2 Minutes

Your site uses Cloudflare Pages Functions located in the functions/api/ folder. To enable persistent storage so your changes save permanently across the globe, connect a KV namespace in your Cloudflare dashboard:

Step 1: Create KV Namespace

  1. Open your Cloudflare Dashboard.
  2. Go to Storage & Databases > KV.
  3. Click Create a namespace and name it PORTAL_DATA.

Step 2: Bind KV to your Pages Project

  1. Go to Workers & Pages > select your JPP-PMKL-STUDENT-PORTAL project.
  2. Click Settings > Functions.
  3. Scroll to KV namespace bindings and click Add binding.
  4. Set Variable name to PORTAL_DATA and select your created KV namespace.
  5. Save and trigger a redeploy (or push any commit to GitHub).

Step 3: Zero Trust Admin Login (Optional & Free)

To require an email verification PIN whenever someone visits admin.html: Go to Zero Trust > Access > Applications > Add yourdomain.com/admin.html and specify your JPP email. Cloudflare will automatically protect the page before anyone can view it!
